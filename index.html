Green Advice 🍃

🍁Indoor Plants

💧 Water wisely – Most indoor plants need water only when the top soil feels dry. Overwatering is the main reason for plant death indoors. ☀️ Give proper light – Place plants near windows with indirect sunlight. Rotate them weekly for even growth. 🌬️ Good airflow – Keep plants where there’s gentle air circulation to prevent fungus and pests. Use well-draining soil – Always use pots with drainage holes to avoid waterlogging. ✨ Clean leaves – Wipe dust off leaves so they can breathe and photosynthesize better. 🌡️ Stable temperature – Avoid placing plants near AC vents, heaters, or direct fans.

🍁Outdoor Plants

🌞 Right sunlight – Some plants love full sun (like succulents), while others prefer partial shade. Group them accordingly. 💧 Regular watering – Outdoor plants dry faster, so water 2–3 times a week (more in summer, less in monsoon/winter). 🌱 Fertilize monthly – Use organic compost or liquid fertilizer every 3–4 weeks for healthy growth. Prune & trim – Remove yellow/dry leaves and prune branches to encourage new growth. 🐛 Check for pests – Inspect regularly for mealybugs, aphids, or mites; treat with neem oil spray. 🪴 Repot when needed – Outdoor plants grow faster, so shift them into bigger pots every year or two.

🍁General Care

Repot plants once a year (when roots outgrow pot). Clean leaves with a damp cloth to help photosynthesis. Check for pests (like mealybugs or aphids) and treat early with neem oil spray.

👉 Quick Memory Rule:

“Right Light + Proper Water + Clean Soil = Healthy Plant” 🌱
